About Cheryl's Company

Bringing professional salon care and empowerment together.

Empowerment & Excellence

Redefining the Salon Experience in Pakistan

Cheryl's Company was established to solve a dual challenge: providing women with safe, luxurious, and accessible beauty services in their own homes, while creating meaningful financial self-sufficiency for skilled female beauty professionals.

Our home salon model eliminates the discomfort of commuting and long wait times in traditional salons, replacing it with personalized, one-on-one attention from background-checked, vetted beauticians.

Social Impact

Supporting Women's Economic Independence

We provide trained female beauty professionals with continuous skill enhancement, dignified work environments, and fair compensation.

Rigorous Vetting

Every artist on our platform undergoes strict identity checks, technical evaluation, and customer service training.

Fair Opportunities

We empower women skilled in beauty trades to achieve sustainable self-sufficiency and financial independence.

Standardized Safety

Our professionals travel equipped with sanitized kits, disposable aprons, and fresh towels for every appointment.

National Reach

Service Coverage

With head offices anchored in DHA Phase 1, Lahore, Cheryl's Company serves client requests across major Pakistani cities including Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Sargodha, Sialkot, Gujranwala, and Bahawalpur.
